# Platform link icon

`zhihu.ico` is the Zhihu favicon downloaded from its official asset endpoint, <https://static.zhihu.com/heifetz/favicon.ico>. It identifies guest links to Zhihu. The logo remains the property of its owner.

Activity links reuse this project's existing `github.svg` and `modelscope.ico` assets for the corresponding platforms.

README contributor lists reuse `github.svg`. `modelscope.png` and `zhihu.png` are PNG format conversions of the existing `modelscope.ico` and `zhihu.ico` logos, retaining their original dimensions and appearance. Platform logos remain the property of their respective owners.
